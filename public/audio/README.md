# Lantern Courtyard

Original composition and synthesized recording created for Hexanexus.
100 BPM; D major / pentatonic; 32 bars; 76.8 seconds; stereo PCM WAV.

All notes and synthesized timbres are defined in scripts/generate-music.mjs.
No third-party recordings, samples, or music from Honkai: Star Rail are used.
The composition and recording may be used, modified, and redistributed with Hexanexus, including inside its APK, without an additional music license or attribution requirement.

The WAV is rendered offline. The app plays the bundled file and does not require a synthesizer or network connection.
